Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Overview

ODD Signal Sync is a personal file synchronization setup using rclone and the Google Drive API. It is operated solely for the owner's use.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google OAuth authorization to access the owner's Google Drive files.

Depending on the granted permissions, this includes listing, reading, creating, modifying and deleting files for synchronization.

Data Handling

Files are synchronized between the owner's local computer and Google Drive.

ODD Signal Sync does not operate an additional server or centrally collect personal data. OAuth credentials are stored locally by rclone.

Data Sharing

No personal data is sold or shared with advertisers. Google processes data as part of providing Google Drive and OAuth services.

Data Retention and Control

Files remain under the owner's control in their local storage and Google Drive account. Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For questions about this application, contact the project owner through the GitHub repository.
